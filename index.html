Try these 3 tips tonight — free

1. Put your phone on your nightstand, not under your pillow.
Mic tracking needs air. Charging cable by the bed helps, it runs all night.
2. Use a smart alarm window, not a fixed time.
Set a 30-minute window. Waking in light sleep feels much easier than a loud alarm in deep sleep.
3. Write down 1 number tomorrow morning.
Just your wake-up feeling, 1–5. After 7 nights you will see a pattern. That is what the log below is for.
